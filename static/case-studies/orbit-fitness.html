Movement and recovery campaign · Fictional Brand / Demo Project

Brief

Develop a conversion-oriented campaign for a fictional daily movement and recovery product, balancing motivation with simple product benefits.

Audience

Active adults building sustainable movement into busy routines.

Creative direction

Natural movement photography, deep forest green, a benefit-led message and a practical CTA designed for mobile placements.

Process

Brief → audience → product promise → art direction → message hierarchy → channel crops → landing-page concept.

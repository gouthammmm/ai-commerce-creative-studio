INDEPENDENT CREATIVE PRACTICE / CASE STUDY

VELORA

Premium lifestyle identity · Fictional Brand / Demo Project · AI-assisted art direction

Brief

Create a distinctive, adaptable identity for a fictional premium lifestyle label, then extend it to product storytelling and social placements.

Audience

People looking for well-made everyday accessories and an understated visual point of view.

Creative direction

Warm stone, muted rose, editorial serif typography and generous image-led layouts, with a repeatable campaign hierarchy.

Process

Brief → audience → identity attributes → typography and palette → product presentation → social, web and email adaptations.

Design development

AI prompt

Premium editorial product campaign for a fictional contemporary lifestyle label: a matte ivory fragrance bottle with a brushed brass cap on travertine, warm late-afternoon light, restrained olive detail, tactile realism and negative space for copy. Generated with Adobe Firefly; no text or logo in the image.

Initial concept

Establish the product or identity as the hero, with a concise headline and enough visual breathing room for the intended audience.

Design refinement

Refine hierarchy, contrast, crop, type scale and CTA so the central message remains clear across mobile and desktop placements.

Final creative

Use the generated product photograph as a campaign master, then pair it with a restrained serif headline, small wordmark and simple CTA. The image is AI-generated for this fictional independent concept.

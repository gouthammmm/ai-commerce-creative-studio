# AURA LIVING — Designed for Living

**Independent commercial design practice · Fictional brand · 2026**

## Brief

Create a warm autumn campaign for a fictional premium home-decor label. The visual work should feel lived-in and tactile, and the eCommerce experience should help people understand the product and move naturally from discovery to a simulated order.

## Objective and audience

Introduce a considered seasonal collection to design-conscious homeowners interested in quality materials, flexible furniture and calm interiors. The practice objective is to show a repeatable process that connects art direction, campaign copy, social placements and commerce UX.

## Creative direction

**Room before object.** Begin with an interior in warm natural light, then use quiet serif headlines, small uppercase wayfinding and a soft olive/stone palette. Keep the CTA functional and legible. Adapt one campaign thought to portrait social, vertical story, wide storefront and email proportions.

## Source image and prompt

The static sample placements use an Unsplash interior render by [Maverick Frame Studio](https://unsplash.com/photos/luxurious-modern-living-room-with-beige-sofa-and-accent-lighting-DgmK-H2VqTU), available there under the regular Unsplash License at the time it was reviewed. The SVGs hotlink that asset; they do not contain generated or original photography. Review the source listing and license before publication or client use.

Image direction used for the campaign concept:

> Premium home-decor campaign for fictional AURA LIVING, softly layered contemporary interior, pale upholstered sofa, textured stone and warm timber, golden-hour light falling across the room, realistic scale and materials, room-led editorial composition, gentle shadow detail, warm restrained palette, quiet luxury, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark, 4:5 portrait crop.

This is an art-direction prompt example. No claim is made that this exact prompt produced the Unsplash image. Optional API output is generated only after the owner configures an image API key.

## Process and adaptation

1. **Brief:** establish the fictional brand, product story, audience and seasonal placement.
2. **Direction:** set the warm editorial art direction and a reusable type/color system.
3. **Copy:** create a campaign headline, support line, description, CTA and platform caption. The no-key app response is an authored demo template.
4. **Image:** use a licensed reference render as the portfolio composition background, with contributor credit; no image-generation result is claimed.
5. **Adapt:** preserve the same campaign voice and hierarchy while changing crop and text position for each SVG format.
6. **Commerce:** connect the campaign to product pages, variants, inventory demo, cart simulation and an order-confirmation state.
7. **Review:** use local SQLite for campaign/assets state and mark every mock integration, simulation and fictional result.

## Results and learning

This is a design/development exercise, not a client engagement. There are no real sales, reach, conversion or campaign results. Any analytics page values besides local demo orders are labeled **Demo / Simulated Data**. The strongest learning was to keep the campaign idea consistent while giving each placement its own crop, reading order and CTA space.
